# Pion Design Lindesberg

## Direction
A colourful textile catalogue built from Christina's own photography and original peony mark. Heritage textile patterns justify DM Serif Display: its generous, rounded letters echo the floral motif. DM Sans makes the dense catalogue easy to use. Berry #8f244c is the sole accent, with white, blush #f5e4e7 and petal #fbeff1 surfaces. All photographs stay upright. No generated photography, stock imagery, invented testimonials or sales urgency.

## Composition
An expansive split hero pairs the pink armchair photograph with a large, clear headline. Product photography carries the visual detail. The catalogue uses an unboxed three-column grid with a category rail; mobile uses two columns and native filters. Product images use contain to preserve the whole object. Below the catalogue, a full-width berry section, peony photograph, ordering guidance and contact block vary the rhythm without decorative clutter.

## Behaviour
215 gallery entries, 15 categories, accent-insensitive search, price/name sorting, progressive loading, native product and inquiry dialogs. Inquiries open the visitor's email app and never claim to place an order. Prices, stock and postage require confirmation by Christina. No analytics, remote fonts, cookies, accounts or payment collection. Inquiry state lives only in memory and clears on reload.

## Responsive and accessibility
Breakpoints 1100, 820 and 600 px. Native buttons, inputs, selects and dialogs, visible focus, meaningful image text, reduced-motion support and live result counts. Desktop body 16 px; mobile keeps the same body size, with smaller secondary product metadata. No scroll hijacking or infinite animation.

## Content provenance
All product images and factual descriptions come from pionlindesberg.com, collected 2 October 2026. Every record retains its source page and original image URL in catalog.js. Product-level tote prices take precedence over conflicting introductory prose; that discrepancy is stated in the product details. About image shows peonies, not a portrait. Product photos can depict variants or multiple items; 215 is an entry count, not an inventory quantity. Font licences ship with the assets.
